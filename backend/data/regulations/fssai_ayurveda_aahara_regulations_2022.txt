Food Safety and Standards (Ayurveda Aahara) Regulations, 2022 - Regulatory Summary

Published in the Gazette of India on 9 May 2022 (notified 5 May 2022) under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006, in consultation with the Ministry of AYUSH.

Definition: "Ayurveda Aahara" means food prepared in accordance with the recipes, ingredients or processes described in the authoritative books of Ayurveda listed under Schedule A of the regulations. It covers recipes and ingredients for promoting health, specific physiological needs, and foods for consumption during or after specified diseases and disorders (Pathya).

Structure of the regulation (verified headings):
1. Short Title and Commencement
2. Definitions
3. General Requirements (Regulation 3: Food Business Operators shall formulate Ayurveda Aahara in accordance with the categories and requirements specified in Schedule B)
4. Additives
5. Contaminants (per FSS (Contaminants, Toxins and Residues) Regulation, 2011)
6. Packaging
7. Restriction on Sale, Manufacture, etc. of Ayurveda Aahara
8. Not to Claim Treating Human Disease, etc. (prohibits labelling, presentation and advertisement claiming the property of preventing, treating or curing any human disease)
9. Claim by Food Business Operator
10. Prior Approval for Ayurveda Aahara (per FSS (Approval for Non-Specific Food and Food Ingredients) Regulation, 2017)
11. Constitution of Expert Committee
12. Labelling of Ayurveda Aahara (intended purpose, target consumer group, recommended duration of use, and other specific requirements)
13. Other Requirements for Ayurveda Aahara
14. Information by the Food Business Operator to Food Authority
Schedules A-E: Schedule A lists the authoritative Ayurvedic books; Schedule B defines the categories of Ayurveda Aahara and their regulatory requirements; Schedule C lists permitted additives; Schedule D sets microbiological standards; Schedule E specifies the mandatory Ayurveda Aahara logo.

Key compliance facts:
- Exclusions: Ayurvedic drugs and proprietary Ayurvedic medicines, medicinal products, cosmetics, narcotic or psychotropic substances, and herbs in Schedule E-1 of the D&C Act 1940/Rules 1945.
- Not recommended for children below 2 years of age.
- Licensing and marketing approval is mandatory (FoSCoS portal); the dedicated logo must be used.
- June/July 2025: FSSAI Order dated 25.07.2025 published the list of Category A Ayurveda Aahara products covered under the 2022 Regulations, providing regulatory clarity and simplifying registration/licensing via FoSCoS for Food Business Operators.
- An Expert Committee constituted with FSSAI and Ministry of AYUSH representatives recommends approval of claims and products and addresses licensing, certification, laboratory accreditation and quality issues.
